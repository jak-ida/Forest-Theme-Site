Cedar Ridge

The cedar forest sanctuary

Step inside

Fern Hollow is a forest sanctuary and woodland lodge in an old cedar grove on Cedar Ridge. We keep the trails quiet, replant slopes that lost their cover, and leave the canopy thicker than we found it.

Stay in a cabin under the moss, walk with a ranger, or spend a weekend planting along the creek — from the nursery beds to the ridge where deer still cross at dusk.
